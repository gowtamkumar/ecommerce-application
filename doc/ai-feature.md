## **1️⃣ Product Recommendations (Upsell / Cross-sell)**

**What it does:** AI suggests products to the user based on their browsing or purchase history.

**Where to add:**

- **Homepage:** “Recommended for you”
- **Product page:** “Customers also bought…”
- **Checkout page:** “You may also like…”

**Example:**
User views a red running shoe → AI suggests: “Red socks, running shorts, shoe cleaner.”

**Benefit:** Increases sales automatically.

---

## **2️⃣ AI Search & Filters**

**What it does:** AI understands what the user wants and gives better search results.

**Where to add:**

- **Search bar**: User types “red running shoes men” → AI finds exact matches.
- **Autocomplete**: Suggest products as the user types.

**Benefit:** Users find products faster → more sales.

---

## **3️⃣ AI Chatbot for Support**

**What it does:** AI answers questions automatically.

**Where to add:**

- **Floating chat widget** on every page
- **Checkout page** to help with orders or issues

**Example:**

- User: “When will my order arrive?”
- AI: “Orders usually arrive in 3–5 days. Track here: [link]”

**Benefit:** Reduces support staff costs and helps users buy confidently.

---

## **4️⃣ AI-Generated Product Descriptions**

**What it does:** AI writes product titles, descriptions, or SEO text automatically.

**Where to add:**

- **Admin dashboard** when adding products

**Example:**

- Input: “Red running shoes, size 8-12, breathable”
- AI Output: “Lightweight Red Running Shoes for comfort and speed—perfect for daily runs.”

**Benefit:** Saves time for you and improves website SEO.

---

## **5️⃣ Personalized Marketing Emails**

**What it does:** AI writes emails or push notifications for users.

**Where to add:**

- **Admin dashboard → Marketing section**
- Example emails:

  - “You liked running shoes, check out our new arrivals!”
  - “Get 20% off on socks you viewed last week.”

**Benefit:** Increases repeat sales automatically.

---

## **6️⃣ Optional Advanced: Visual Search**

**What it does:** AI finds products from photos users upload.

**Where to add:**

- **Search page / mobile app camera feature**
- User uploads a photo of a shoe → AI finds similar shoes in your store

**Benefit:** Modern shopping experience → higher user engagement
